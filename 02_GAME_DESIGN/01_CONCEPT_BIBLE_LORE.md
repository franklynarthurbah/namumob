# Concept bible, core loop, lore, glossary
> Read after: 00_START_HERE · Used in: all phases (intent reference), Phase 5 (UI copy), Phase 7 (map dressing)

## 1. Pitch
**NAMULINDA** — *Loot. Record. Extract.* You are a Runner sent into a dark, overgrown megacity-basin to salvage what the old world left behind. Your Kestrel bodycam is your only eye: the world is seen through its lens, and everything you do is recorded. Ride stunt bikes across a huge basin, out-think AI garrisons, dodge rival squads, and get out before the Dimming swallows the map.

## 2. Audience and sessions
Mid-core mobile shooter players who like extraction tension, plus stunt/vehicle fans. Sessions: **Skirmish** 12 min, **Run** 20–25 min, **Arena** 5–8 min, **Freewheel** free-roam stunt practice. Daily loop: contracts → 1–3 raids → Safehouse upgrades → cosmetics.

## 3. Core loop
```
Safehouse (stash, traders, workshop) → choose kit + bracket → squad up → Dropship
→ Drop → Loot / ride / fight AI + squads → Extraction decision (greed vs exit)
→ Extract (keep loot)  OR  die (lose carried gear, keep Safe Case + insured items)
→ Settlement (profit ledger, XP, Feed Rep, Reel) → Safehouse
```
Metagame: Scrip → better kits/upgrades → riskier brackets → higher yields. Cosmetics (Cred) never change power.

## 4. Lore (fictional; no real places or groups)
- **The basin:** Lake Verra and the city of **Namulinda**, once home to 9 million people, laced with the **Halo Grid**, a wireless power/data mesh.
- **The Dimming (2041):** a cascade failure blacked out the Grid. The city was sealed and left. Nature took the edges; the core sits dark.
- **Eight years later:** the **Salvage Concession Board (SCB)** licenses **Runners** to retrieve valuables. Every Runner wears a **Kestrel** bodycam that streams to **The Feed**, a public broadcast that funds the licences. Your footage is proof-of-loot and entertainment.
- **The Dimming today:** dead Grid nodes still pulse. Zones of blackout drift outward when nodes wake, degrading signal, sanity and equipment.

## 5. Factions (all AI factions are fictional)
| Faction | Role | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Scrap Crews** | Desperate scavengers, tier 1 | patched clothing, improvised/old weapons, hand-painted markings |
| **Concession Guards** | Ex-SCB security gone private, tier 2 | mismatched tactical gear, orange armbands, squads of 4, use flanking and grenades |
| **Halo Remnants** | Leftover automated defence, tier 3 | drones, sentries, light-blue status LEDs, EMP pulses |
| **Traders** | Safehouse NPCs | **Fixer** (weapons), **Doc** (medical), **Wrench** (mods, bikes, vehicles), **Broker** (buy/sell, contracts) |
Tone: resilient, community-minded, hopeful-gritty. Avoid poverty-tourism or "warlord" tropes; cast and voices are diverse and dignified.

## 6. Visual identity in one paragraph
Laterite red earth, sun-bleached concrete, dense green regrowth, warm tungsten pockets against blue-white Halo LEDs. Feed cyan and REC red are reserved for the bodycam UI.

## 7. Glossary
Run/Raid · **Dimming** (hazard front) · **Feed** (broadcast/HUD identity) · **Kestrel** (bodycam) · **Scrip** (earned currency) · **Cred** (Feed Credits, purchased) · **Safe Case** · **Body Bag** (death container) · **Send-Off Ramp** (stunt extraction) · **Hype** (trick score) · **Boost** (nitro) · **Aptitudes** (permanent small bonuses) · **Safehouse** (hub) · **Reels** (clips) · **Gear Value** (bracket metric) · **Signal Cache** (airdrop) · **Last Bird** (final extraction).

## 8. Content guardrails and rating
Target 16+/Teen: realistic gun violence, mild blood, no dismemberment, no torture, no gambling, no real-world conflict references. Chat features need reporting/muting. Story is told via radio logs, contracts and environments; no long cutscenes.
